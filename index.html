AI & LLMs

Claude text watermarking expands to older models, effective today

Anthropic emailed API customers that from 3 November, output from Claude Mythos 5, Opus 4.7, Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 will carry a detectable watermark, with Amazon Bedrock a few days behind. It is Anthropic’s implementation of SynthID-Text, the Google DeepMind technique published in Nature in 2024 — a statistical signal in token sampling, not an invisible character or metadata tag. A watermark shows text was written or processed by Claude; its absence does not prove a human wrote it.

CNN Business

Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon debuts, but access is locked down

Announced 28 October as Google’s strongest model, Carbon is pitched at cybersecurity and long-duration agentic tasks. Rather than a broad launch, Google is releasing it first to a subset of cyber-defenders under its Fairwind program, with no date for wider availability — a deliberately restricted rollout that contrasts with OpenAI’s cheaper, broadly-available GPT-6.1 Terra from 26 October.

Automotive & MBD

BMW brings Level 2 driver assist into cities, standard-fit from 2027

BMW said (2 November) it will extend its driver-assistance system to urban driving, with the required sensor/compute hardware becoming standard on key model lines from 2027. Rollout starts in Germany pending final regulatory sign-off expected early 2027, then other markets. BMW is explicit that this remains SAE Level 2 — hands-on, driver-responsible — not autonomous driving, positioning it against Tesla’s supervised FSD and Mercedes’ L2++ stack.

Automotive News

Tesla adds Fort Worth to its robotaxi network; NHTSA probes school-zone speeding

Tesla launched robotaxi service in Fort Worth on 2 November, extending its Texas footprint alongside Austin, Dallas and Houston. Separately, NHTSA is reviewing a complaint alleging the automated driving system sped in school zones, covering both Model Y and Cybercab robotaxis. Consumer FSD (v14.3.x as of 31 October) remains supervised.

Safety and cybersecurity now have to be engineered together (MBD angle)

An Electronics Weekly analysis argues ISO 26262 (functional safety) and ISO/SAE 21434 (cybersecurity) can no longer be separate workstreams: AUTOSAR’s SecOC adds per-frame authentication and a freshness counter, and the piece quantifies the crypto latency this adds on a safety-critical braking ECU handling an ADAS camera command — a direct concern for MBD teams budgeting timing on secured signal paths. (analysis, late October — not new-this-week)
