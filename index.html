AI & LLMs

Anthropic previews Claude Duet, a proactive personal agent

Announced 21 October, Claude Duet is a research-preview app that works across your connected apps (email, calendar, docs) in the background and reaches out first with suggestions, rather than only answering prompts. It is built on Claude Opus 5.5 and is gated to Pro and Max subscribers via a waitlist, positioning Anthropic against consumer-agent startups such as Muse and Dots.

cnbc.com

Anthropic donates the Agent Skills standard to the Linux Foundation

On 22 October Anthropic handed its Agent Skills standard — the open “SKILL.md” format that teaches an agent a task or domain, which grew out of Claude Code — to the new Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation, including the name, logo, GitHub org and agentskills.io domain. Engineers from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI will maintain it as individuals; next steps include versioned releases and security guidance for skill authors.

anthropic.com

Anthropic opens a life-sciences guest-investigator program

Also on 22 October, Anthropic put out an open call for guest investigators in the life sciences, inviting researchers to apply for special projects — part of a widening push (alongside its earlier Genesis Mission science commitment) to apply frontier models to scientific R&D.

anthropic.com/news

GPT-6.1 “Terra” surfaces in a Microsoft code leak (rumour — not officially announced)

Testing Catalog reports that a model dubbed GPT-6.1 Terra appeared in a Microsoft pull request, said to beat OpenAI’s GPT-6.1 Sol while costing less; the same reporting points to a Google “Gemini 4 Carbon” in post-training. Neither is officially confirmed — treat as leak until OpenAI or Google announces.

testingcatalog.com

Automotive & MBD

Tesla pushes unsupervised consumer FSD to Q2 2027

On its 21 October Q3 2026 earnings call, Tesla moved the target for unsupervised FSD in customer-owned cars to Q2 2027, and said it will arrive only by adding owners’ vehicles into its geofenced Robotaxi zones rather than as a blanket over-the-air unlock. Musk reiterated that robotaxi and unsupervised FSD will not contribute meaningfully to 2026 financials, anchoring the inflection to 2027.

electrek.co

Mercedes-Benz to bring Drive Assist Pro to Australia as an FSD rival

Mercedes-Benz’s head of active safety says its Drive Assist Pro hands-free system will reach Australia within two years, to challenge Tesla FSD. The system fuses 10 cameras with five radars and 12 ultrasonic sensors, leaning on radar/ultrasonics at night or in poor weather — a sensor-fusion and validation stack squarely in the MBD/ISO 26262 world an OEM controls engineer would work on.

drive.com.au

BYD extends “God’s Eye” driver assistance down the range

BYD is rolling out its tiered “God’s Eye” driver-assist suite across more of its line-up over the next two to three years: the entry tier uses cameras, ultrasonic sensors and radar with cloud support for freeway driving, while higher tiers add LiDAR — part of a push to make L2+ assistance standard rather than a premium option.

carsguide.com.au

India · Cars & Bikes

Benelli TRK 552 and 552X launch at ₹7.22–7.74 lakh

Benelli launched its new mid-weight adventure twins in India around 22 October: the road-biased TRK 552 at ₹7.22 lakh and the more off-road TRK 552X at ₹7.74 lakh (ex-showroom), with a limited edition at ₹7.90 lakh. They run a 554cc parallel-twin (~52–54 bhp, 49 Nm) with a TFT console, slotting under the popular TRK 702 range.

rushlane.com

Ducati launches the new Hypermotard V2 and V2 SP

Ducati has brought its updated Hypermotard V2 and V2 SP supermotos to India, in line with its stated plan to introduce them by the end of October. The V2 is listed from roughly ₹16.5 lakh (ex-showroom) — a lighter, V2-engined replacement for the outgoing 950, aimed at the premium naked/supermoto niche.

bikewale.com

Hyundai Bayon due for 26 October launch (upcoming — price not yet official)

Hyundai will launch the Bayon crossover in India on 26 October, with bookings open at ₹11,000 and dealer stock already reaching showrooms. Media price estimates run roughly ₹9.5–15 lakh — treat as estimate until the official launch-day figure. The India-spec car is expected to use the Creta’s 1.5L naturally aspirated petrol.
