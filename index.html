AI & LLMs

Anthropic’s draft S-1 leaks ahead of a November IPO (leaked draft — figures via secondary reporting, not the final prospectus)

A cover page and figures from Anthropic’s confidential S-1 (first filed with the SEC on 1 June) have surfaced online. Secondary reports of the draft cite roughly $16.3bn in H1 2026 revenue (Q1 ~$4.7bn, Q2 ~$10.9–11.5bn depending on the source), about 25% of it from just two unnamed customers, and a large, mostly non-cash H1 loss. A Goldman/Morgan Stanley-led listing is reportedly targeted for November; the last private round valued the firm at ~$965bn, with IPO talk running toward $2tn.

OpenAI pursues a ~$30bn bridge round at a ~$1.4tn pre-money valuation (reported talks — terms unsettled)

Bloomberg-sourced reporting has OpenAI in talks for a round of up to $30bn, anchored by UAE funds (Abu Dhabi’s MGX, possibly up to $10bn) and with BlackRock in discussions, at a reported ~$1.4tn pre-money valuation — up ~64% from the $852bn March round. It is framed as a bridge that lets OpenAI delay its own IPO while Anthropic moves first. No lead investor is set and terms could change.

thenextweb.com

Anthropic donates the Agent Skills standard to the new Agentic AI Foundation

On 22 October Anthropic handed its open “SKILL.md” Agent Skills format — the standard for teaching an agent a task or domain, which grew out of Claude Code — to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation, including the name, logo, GitHub org and domain. Engineers from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI will maintain it as individuals.

Automotive & MBD

Toyota recalls the 2026 RAV4 PHEV over a hybrid-ECU software fault

An initialisation-sequence error in the hybrid ECU can leave the SUV unable to engage the modes needed for high-speed driving: a rear-motor fault drops it into a fail-safe front-wheel-drive limp mode, while a front-unit fault at highway speed can shut the whole plug-in system down. The campaign covers ~30,978 units in the US, ~7,846 in Canada and ~5,925 in Australia (Japan-built PHEVs); the fix is a free hybrid-ECU software reflash, with no crashes reported. A textbook MIL/SIL-validation and ISO 26262 fail-safe case.

autoevolution.com

Automotive software recalls hit a record for the sixth year running

S&P Global Mobility reports that vehicle recalls traced to software defects reached an all-time high in 2025 and kept climbing — the sixth consecutive record year — as electronics and OTA-updatable control units become the dominant failure mode. The trend underlines why model-based development, functional-safety process and rigorous SIL/MIL verification are now front-line OEM concerns, not back-office ones.

spglobal.com

Tesla’s first Robotaxi Safety Report claims 3.1M driverless miles, zero injuries

Published alongside the 21 October Q3 call, the report logs 3.1 million autonomous miles without a safety monitor between 22 January and 18 October 2026, with zero reported injuries or airbag deployments — against Waymo’s 271M+ cumulative autonomous miles. On the same call Tesla pushed unsupervised FSD in customer-owned cars to Q2 2027, and only by folding owners’ vehicles into its geofenced Robotaxi zones.
