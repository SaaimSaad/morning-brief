Anthropic launches Claude Whiteboards, Sheets and Data

On 10 Nov Anthropic opened Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta and made its dashboards product generally available, renamed Claude Data. On Team plans all three are on by default; Enterprise starts with Whiteboards and Sheets off, with Data scheduled to switch on around 19–20 Nov. Automotive & MBD

NHTSA probes Waymo after robotaxis keep passing stopped school buses

US regulator NHTSA opened an investigation (filing dated ~6 Nov) after Waymo vehicles continued illegally passing stopped school buses even after a software update meant to fix the behaviour — most logged incidents reportedly came after the fix. A pointed reminder for safety engineers that an OTA patch is not verified closure: the regression surfaced in the field, not in validation. (agency filing + TechCrunch reporting; Waymo says it has had no citations since March 2026)

On Texas’s autonomous-vehicle registry Tesla listed roughly 1,302 vehicles against Waymo’s ~1,297 as of about 10 Nov — reportedly the first time Tesla’s registered fleet has topped Waymo’s in the state, as both scale driverless operations and Tesla expands beyond Austin. (figures come from a state registry via secondary trackers and shift day to day; counts differ slightly by source)

Autocar India

Record October for India’s e-scooters; TVS on top, Ola slides

Electric two-wheeler sales hit a record ~223,800 units in October (+48% YoY). TVS led with a record 60,429 units (~27% share, up ~94% YoY), Bajaj was second at ~49,100 (~22%), while Ather slipped to ~14% share (from 20% a year ago) and Ola Electric collapsed to ~5% from 11%. Hero Vida (~29,900) is now within ~1,900 units of Ather. (retail vs Vahan-registration figures vary slightly by source)
