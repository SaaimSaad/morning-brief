Anthropic prices its IPO today; first Nasdaq trade Thursday as “ANT”

Anthropic’s amended S-1 offers 825 million Class A shares at $70–$78, implying a market value of roughly $1.3–1.45 trillion; pricing is expected after the US close on Wednesday and trading begins Thursday. CNBC and Yahoo report the book is heavily oversubscribed, with bankers likely to price at or above the top of the range. (not yet priced as of this edition; the oversubscription and “above range” read are from reporting, not the filing — the final number can still move)

Yahoo Finance

Nvidia blows past estimates — $115.8B revenue, $130B guide — in first print since Huang’s exit

Nvidia reported fiscal-Q3 (quarter ended 25 Oct) revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% YoY, with Data Center alone at $107.6 billion (+110%); it guided Q4 to $130 billion ±3% and called the Vera Rubin NVL72 its fastest-ever production ramp. It was the company’s first earnings since founder Jensen Huang abruptly resigned as CEO on 11 Nov amid a scandal, with CFO Colette Kress now interim chief. (numbers from Nvidia’s release; the Q4 guide assumes zero China data-center compute revenue)

NVIDIA Newsroom

OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 “Astra” and “Sol” with lower API prices

OpenAI released two models on 17 Nov: GPT-6.2 Astra (pitched at complex professional work, $8/$40 per 1M input/output tokens) and the cheaper Sol ($2/$10), both live in ChatGPT and the API. OpenAI’s system card classifies them at its “Critical” cybersecurity-capability threshold, and reports say Astra is the first released model to reach the “High” bar for AI self-improvement. (benchmark gains are OpenAI’s own; capability-threshold claims from the system card and secondary coverage)

The Decoder

$26 billion chip-and-cloud placement rides alongside the Anthropic listing

In a concurrent private placement, seven firms — AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC — have agreed to buy an aggregate $26.0 billion of non-voting Class N stock at the IPO price, reportedly with Nvidia and Google in for ~$10 billion each. It closes just after the IPO and is not a condition of it. (per-firm split is from secondary coverage; placement subject to closing conditions incl. stockholder approval)

Automotive & MBD

Mercedes launches hands-free, point-to-point L2 assist to take on Tesla FSD

Mercedes-Benz is rolling out “Hands-Free Assist” and “Drive Assist Pro,” a Level 2 system that handles point-to-point highway driving hands-off at up to 130 km/h, positioned directly against Tesla’s FSD. Launch begins in Germany this year, with the US following in 2027. (reported 17 Nov; this is a hands-off L2 feature — the driver stays responsible, not an eyes-off/L3 system)

Automotive News

AI agents move into the AUTOSAR toolchain

An engineering feature details work building AI pipelines on top of AUTOSAR — prototyping agents that parse AUTOSAR interface descriptions and auto-generate edge-case test vectors to ease integration, test and ISO 26262 compliance. It’s a concrete look at where AI is actually entering MBD/embedded workflows, rather than the vehicle itself — squarely in your lane. (vendor engineering piece, German-language; recent but undated)

Qualcomm pitches the “AI-Defined Vehicle” as the next step past SDV

At the European Automotive Summit in Munich, Qualcomm — with Renault and Mercedes-Benz — outlined an “AI-Defined Vehicle” stage in which on-board AI agents coordinate multiple vehicle functions, keeping the software-defined vehicle as the technical base layer. (conference framing from ~1 week ago, German-language source; direction-of-travel, not a shipping product)
