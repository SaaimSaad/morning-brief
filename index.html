AI & LLMs

Anthropic sets IPO price band: $70–$78, trading from 19 Nov

Anthropic’s amended SEC filing (10 Nov) sets a $70–$78 range for 825 million Class A shares (about 801 million new, 24 million from existing holders) — raising up to ~$64 billion and implying a fully-diluted valuation near $1.3–1.4 trillion. Pricing is reported for 18 Nov and first Nasdaq trade under “ANT” on 19 Nov, with ~20% of stock set aside for retail. (range and dates are reported pre-pricing; the IPO has not priced yet and Anthropic hasn’t confirmed a final number)

FinanceFeeds

TSMC backs both Anthropic and OpenAI; OpenAI closes a round at ~$1.25T

TSMC’s board reportedly approved up to $1 billion in investment split evenly between Anthropic and OpenAI — a rare move by the foundry into its own AI customers. Separately, OpenAI is reported to be closing a funding round valuing it at about $1.25 trillion, though the raise (cited at ~$11–15 billion) is well below its original ~$30 billion target. (both figures are reported/second-hand and not confirmed by the companies — treat with caution)

Tech Funding News

Anthropic puts self-hosted “Claude Managed Agents” into private beta

Anthropic reportedly opened a private beta (12 Nov) of a self-hosted edition of Claude Managed Agents, letting enterprises run the full agent harness — sandboxes, a PostgreSQL session store and workers — inside their own cloud or on-prem rather than Anthropic’s. Relevant for regulated industries (automotive among them) that can’t send code or data off-site. (reported via AI-news aggregators; details not independently confirmed against an Anthropic release)

Automotive & MBD

Infineon unveils AURIX RC1 for software-defined vehicles and “Physical AI”

At electronica (Munich, 10–13 Nov) Infineon introduced the AURIX RC1 family concept — a next-gen real-time compute/control MCU for SDV and zonal architectures, rated to ASIL D with ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity built in, with samples targeted for H1 2027. Infineon also gave a first look at a separate automotive MCU platform built on open RISC-V. Squarely in the AUTOSAR-Classic-plus-functional-safety space you work in as compute consolidates onto fewer, higher-performance MCUs. (RC1 is a concept with 2027 samples, not yet in production)

EE Journal

Infineon and Qualcomm to co-build central car computers

The two agreed to jointly build central car computers for automated driving and digital cockpits, pairing Infineon’s safety, connectivity and memory subsystem with Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Ride SoCs. Infineon’s safety MCU sits beside the Snapdragon SoC, monitors its health and manages safety mechanisms — a concrete example of the mixed-criticality / freedom-from-interference partitioning that zonal E/E architectures lean on. (partnership announced around electronica; shipping products not yet dated)

EE Journal

BYD’s Yangwang Altior debuts with an in-house assisted-driving chip

BYD’s ultra-luxury Yangwang brand revealed the Altior at a global premiere in Abu Dhabi (7 Nov) — a quad-motor electric Rolls-Royce rival claiming 1,000km-plus CLTC range, and notably the first BYD model to run its own in-house “Xuanji A3” assisted-driving chip. A marker of OEMs vertically integrating ADAS compute rather than buying it from Nvidia/Qualcomm. (range and price figures are vendor claims pre-homologation; pricing varies by source)

CarWale

India’s E2W market up ~48% YoY in October, but share slips

Electric two-wheeler sales reached roughly 224,000 units in October 2026, up about 48% year-on-year (JMK Research). Even so, EVs’ share of the overall two-wheeler market dipped as GST cuts on petrol models revived ICE demand — a reminder that fiscal policy, not just product, is steering the mix. (volumes from a monthly research report; share figures firm up as final data lands)
