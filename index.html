AI & LLMs

Amazon nears a ~$7B+ deal for AI startup Decart — a bet on chip efficiency

Reports (first the WSJ, then Israeli outlets Calcalist and Globes) say Amazon is close to buying Decart in a cash-and-stock deal worth more than $7 billion, rising toward ~$7.5 billion if targets are met. Annapurna Labs chief Nafea Bshara — the team behind AWS’s Trainium silicon — is said to be leading it, the logic being Decart’s chip-optimisation software to lift Trainium efficiency and ease moving models across hardware. Decart raised $300M+ in May (Nvidia among backers) at just under $4B and makes the Lucy video model and Oasis world model. (reports, not a signed deal; notably, Anthropic explored a ~$6B Decart buy in August then walked away after due diligence)

TechRepublic

Alibaba ships Qwen4-Max — 2.4T-parameter MoE, 1M context, well under Western pricing

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max (~16 Nov): a mixture-of-experts model with 2.4 trillion total parameters, a 1 million-token context window and multimodal input (text, image, video), text out. API pricing is $1.50 input / $4.50 output per million tokens — down from Qwen3.8-Max’s $2/$6 and far below GPT-6-class flagships — with cached input at $0.075/M. (figures from third-party reporting, not Alibaba’s own pages; sources disagree on the exact date, with an 11 Nov preview and 15/16 Nov launch cited)

DataCamp

CrowdStrike: China-linked crew ran AI agents against 200+ orgs — agentic hacking at scale

A China-nexus group CrowdStrike tracks as “VAPOR PANDA” used ARTEX, a multi-agent framework (a lead agent directing planning and worker sub-agents), against 200+ organisations across 14+ countries in 11 days from 17 October — foreign ministries, semiconductor, defence and think tanks — running mainly on China’s DeepSeek LLM plus one unconfirmed commercial model. Post-exploitation was confirmed at nine. (CrowdStrike report dated ~29 Oct, not fresh this weekend — included as the clearest real-world case so far of agentic AI turned to offensive use)

Automotive & MBD

Pony.ai and Toyota start a fully driverless robotaxi service in Guangzhou

On 16 November Pony.ai and Toyota launched fully driverless commercial robotaxi operations in Guangzhou using the purpose-built, Gen-7 bZ4X-based robotaxi — an OEM-plus-AV pairing reaching no-safety-driver Level 4 service in a major Chinese city. (a production OEM platform, not a one-off pilot vehicle — the kind of validated-at-scale deployment that pulls safety and SOTIF work to the front)

AutoConnectedCar

Mercedes-Benz brings point-to-point MB.Drive Assist Pro to the US

Mercedes-Benz is rolling out its MB.Drive Assist Pro point-to-point ADAS in the United States, starting with an Early Access Program in select California cities from December 2026 — door-to-door assisted driving that stitches together highway and urban assistance rather than a single-ODD feature. (early-access rollout, geographically limited at launch)

AutoConnectedCar

Tesla’s FSD (Supervised) cleared in Italy — the 12th EU country

Italy approved Tesla’s supervised assisted-driving system (formerly FSD), making it the 12th EU country to allow it, with a possible EU-wide vote to follow in December. It keeps the system firmly in eyes-on, hands-ready L2+ territory under European type-approval. (country-level approval of a supervised system; not eyes-off autonomy)

India · Cars & Bikes

Denza (BYD) launches the Z9 GT at ₹1.57 crore, plus the D9 MPV at ~₹90 lakh

BYD’s luxury brand Denza launched in India on 13 November: the Z9 GT shooting brake at ₹1.57 crore (ex-showroom) — tri-motor AWD, 0–100 km/h in a claimed 2.7 sec, ~670 km NEDC range — sold as a full import in just six cities (Mumbai, Gurugram, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad), bookings at ₹10 lakh. The D9 luxury MPV launched alongside at about ₹90 lakh. (CBU imports; range is NEDC, which flatters real-world figures)

Autocar India

Toyota teases the Innova Hycross facelift — launches Wednesday, likely India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid

Toyota released its first teaser of the facelifted Innova Hycross ahead of a 25 November launch. It is widely expected to be India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid — a petrol-electric hybrid able to run on blends up to ~E85 ethanol — which would be a notable powertrain-engineering first for the market. (launch date confirmed by teaser coverage; the flex-fuel hybrid is expected, not yet officially confirmed, and the supported ethanol blend is unstated)

Autocar India

Bengaluru AI-chip startup Agrani Labs raises $8M seed led by Peak XV

Bengaluru-based Agrani Labs raised $8 million (~₹73.4 crore) in a seed round led by Peak XV Partners, to build AI GPUs and the surrounding software for data-centre compute — part of a wider Indian deep-tech push into homegrown AI silicon. (reported this month; a seed-stage bet, with first silicon still some way off)
