AI & LLMs

OpenAI reportedly nears a ~$30bn raise at a ~$1.25 trillion valuation (reported / in talks)

CNBC reported on 24 October that OpenAI is closing in on a funding round of up to $30bn at roughly a $1.25 trillion valuation — even as an earlier revenue disclosure knocked AI-infrastructure stocks (Nvidia, Oracle, CoreWeave) earlier in the month. Separately, Anthropic, which confidentially filed an IPO prospectus with the SEC in June, is seen as the only major AI firm that might go public before year-end. Treat the OpenAI figure as a report, not a closed deal.

cnbc.com

Mistral’s trillion-parameter “Le Chonk” open weights land this week

Mistral Large 4 — a mixture-of-experts model of about 1.05 trillion parameters (~52bn active per token) — is set to publish its open weights on 27 October, after a roughly three-week guardrail-only test period. API pricing runs about $1.36 per million input tokens and $4.18 output. Mistral calls it the strongest open-weight model built outside China; most benchmark figures so far are Mistral’s own. Open weights matter if you want to run or fine-tune a frontier-class model on your own hardware.

venturebeat.com

Anthropic hands its “Agent Skills” standard to a neutral foundation

On 22 October Anthropic donated Agent Skills — its open format for packaging reusable agent knowledge and workflows — to the Agentic AI Foundation, saying more than 70 agent platforms have already integrated it. A small but telling move to standardise how AI agents are handed domain procedures: the kind of plumbing that matters when you try to wire AI into an engineering toolchain rather than a chat window.

anthropic.com

Automotive & MBD

Tesla reverses on maps and pushes unsupervised FSD to 2027

Tesla now says it is building its own maps to fix FSD’s handling of speed limits, parking and route choice — after years of insisting it needed none. It also moved unsupervised FSD in privately-owned cars to Q2 2027, and only by enrolling those cars into its small, geofenced Robotaxi fleet; California still requires a safety driver. Q3 numbers: FSD subscriptions up 67% year-on-year to 1.74 million.

electrek.co

NHTSA proposes marker lights so you can tell a car is driving itself

In its 25 October mobility briefing, TechCrunch reports NHTSA has proposed new lighting requirements for autonomous vehicles — including an option for cyan (blue-green) lights to signal when a vehicle is operating autonomously with no human monitoring the road. A rare US federal step toward a standard external HMI signal for driverless operation; still only a proposal at this stage.

techcrunch.com

Waymo keeps scaling: 4,000+ driverless cars, airports, Madrid next

Waymo now runs more than 4,000 driverless vehicles in US commercial service, began airport pickups and drop-offs in Las Vegas (dedicated AV zones at Terminals 1 and 3) around 21 October, and says it will open a robotaxi service in Madrid — its first Spanish city — to riders in 2027. On the ground, the gap between mapped-and-lidar and vision-only approaches keeps widening.

India · Cars & Bikes

Hyundai Bayon launches in India today

Hyundai launches the Bayon — a 4.2m crossover slotting between Venue and Creta — in India today, 26 October. It uses the Creta’s 1.5-litre MPi petrol (115 PS / 143.8 Nm) with a 6-speed manual or iVT; no diesel or turbo, and a CNG variant is expected only later. Bookings opened at ₹11,000; media estimates put it around ₹9.5–15 lakh ex-showroom, with official prices due today.
