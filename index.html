Anthropic IPO enters final stretch: pricing 18 Nov, Nasdaq debut 19 Nov as “ANT”

With the roadshow that opened 10 Nov now near its close, Anthropic is offering 825 million Class A shares (about 801 million new) at $70–$78, implying ~$64 billion raised and a fully-diluted valuation near $1.4 trillion; underwriters hold a 30-day option on up to ~124 million more shares. TechCrunch

Google ships agent-to-agent plumbing for Gemini Enterprise

Google’s Gemini Enterprise release notes (13 Nov) add support for A2A agents built with the Agent Development Kit and deployed on Agent Runtime to authenticate to downstream Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers — concrete, production-grade wiring for multi-agent systems rather than a model headline. Relevant if you’re eyeing agentic tooling for engineering workflows. (primary-source Google Cloud release note)

Automotive & MBD

Qualcomm and Infineon widen Snapdragon Ride for central SDV compute

Ahead of electronica in Munich, Qualcomm and Infineon expanded their partnership to speed up central car computers for software-defined vehicles — pairing Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Ride SoCs with Infineon’s safety, power, connectivity and memory silicon for centralized zonal architectures. It is the clearest signal this week of the industry consolidating ECUs into a few high-performance compute domains, the shift reshaping how embedded control software is partitioned and deployed. (confirmed partnership announcement, ~3 Nov; timed to electronica)

Electronics Media

Tesla robotaxi goes 24/7 across all eight US cities; Cybercab on sale

Tesla says its robotaxi service now runs around the clock in every city where it operates, having dropped the overnight pause, and it launched in San Antonio on 9 Nov with a service area of 270+ sq mi (vs ~60 for Waymo there) though starting with only a handful of cars. Tesla also listed the Cybercab for sale with a mandatory $299/month “Autonomy Subscription,” deliveries possibly from December. (Teslarati/TechCrunch reporting; Cybercab delivery timing and the real driverless-vs-registered fleet size remain unconfirmed)
