Anthropic reportedly used Claude agents to rewrite its API in Rust, cutting CPU use ~70%

Automotive & MBD

BMW won’t offer Level 3 on the facelifted 7 Series — says buyers aren’t interested

BMW is holding its eyes-off Level 3 assistant off the updated 7 Series, with its development chief citing weak demand and buyers’ unwillingness to pay the premium, while keeping the tech in development. It underscores the Western OEM retreat to “Level 2++” where the driver stays liable. (reported 23 November; the L2++ vs L3 liability/cost trade-off is the strategic backdrop for any OEM ADAS programme)

BMWBlog

China moves first on Level 3 highway driving — BAIC/Huawei Stelato begins tests at 120 km/h

On 17 November the BAIC×Huawei Stelato brand began L3 highway testing on the Stelato V8, capable of L3 driving at 120 km/h with lane changes and ramp deceleration; China’s first conditional L3 permits went to Changan and BAIC for designated zones in Beijing and Chongqing. (official test launch; permits are geo-fenced and speed-limited, not open-road L3)

CarNewsChina

Mercedes-Benz sets December US launch for MB.Drive Assist Pro — subscription L2++ point-to-point

Mercedes’ point-to-point hands-free ADAS begins an Early Access Program in select California cities in December 2026, framed as a pivot from geo-fenced SAE Level 3 toward subscription-based Level 2++ highway autonomy — keeping the driver legally responsible. (the redundancy needed for eyes-off L3 is the functional-safety crux Mercedes is sidestepping for now)

AutoConnectedCar

US robotaxi race tightens — Tesla reaches its 8th city, trades the Texas fleet lead with Waymo

Tesla’s unsupervised robotaxi service expanded to San Antonio (its 5th Texas market; Austin now ~443 sq mi), and on the Texas DMV registry Tesla led with 1,507 registered robotaxis to Waymo’s 1,321 as of 18 November after a batch of 200 Cybercabs. (fleet counts come from state registry trackers, not company filings; Waymo issued a late-November recall tied to handling human instructions)

India · Cars & Bikes

Royal Enfield Himalayan 750 launches today at Motoverse, Goa

Royal Enfield opened India bookings for the Himalayan 750 on 27 November with the price revealed at Motoverse (Goa, 27–29 November). The all-new 743cc parallel-twin — its biggest engine yet — makes ~55 bhp at 7,000 rpm and 63 Nm at 5,000 rpm, sitting above the Himalayan 450; UK pricing starts at £6,999 and India was widely expected around ₹4–5 lakh. (launch/price reveal is scheduled at today’s event; confirm the final on-road price against Royal Enfield before relying on it)

BikeDekho

Toyota Innova HyCross Flex Hybrid now on sale — India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid, from ₹19.99 lakh

Toyota Kirloskar has confirmed the Innova HyCross Flex Hybrid on sale from ₹19.99 lakh (ex-showroom), pairing its 2.0-litre petrol strong-hybrid powertrain with an engine certified for high ethanol blends — a genuine powertrain-engineering first for the market and a real test of ethanol strong-hybrids at scale. (now officially launched per EVreporter; a car, not a BEV despite the branding)

EVreporter

VinFast to enter India’s electric two-wheeler market with mass-market models in early 2027

Vietnam’s VinFast plans mass-market electric scooters for India in early 2027 — a timeline pushed back from an earlier second-half-2026 target — adding a new entrant to a crowded e-2W field led by Ola, TVS, Ather and Bajaj. (company plan; VinFast has not yet named an India-specific product lineup or firm launch date)
