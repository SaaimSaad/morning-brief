AI & LLMs

Anthropic prices IPO at $83, debuts on Nasdaq today as “ANT” — a record $81.3B raise

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on Wednesday, above the $70–$78 range, and upsized the deal to roughly 980 million shares (from 825 million) to raise about $81.3 billion — the biggest IPO on record, topping SpaceX’s ~$75 billion. That implies a fully-diluted valuation near $1.5 trillion. First trade is today, Thursday 19 November. (price and raise are consistent across outlets; first-day trading performance is not yet known as of this edition, and the valuation basis varies — ~$1.08T market cap vs ~$1.5T fully diluted)

CNN

Strategic buyers take ~$26B of Anthropic stock in a concurrent private placement

Alongside the public offering, a group of strategic partners — reported to include Nvidia, Google, Microsoft and Broadcom — agreed to buy about $26 billion of non-voting stock at the $83 IPO price, lifting the combined deal to roughly $94.5 billion. The placement is separate from, and not a condition of, the IPO. (per-buyer split is from secondary coverage; placement subject to closing conditions)

Automotive & MBD

California pulls Waymo’s driverless robotaxis off freeways; Waymo halts highway rides everywhere

A California DMV order barring Waymo’s driverless vehicles from state freeways took effect 11 November and became public on the 17th; in response Waymo temporarily suspended driverless freeway rides across all its markets. Separately, federal regulators (NHTSA) are probing whether Waymo vehicles reliably detect and stop for stopped school buses. (details from secondary coverage; a reminder that the hard part of autonomy is edge-case perception and regulatory validation, not the happy path)

The Truth About Cars

Tesla’s Robotaxi keeps expanding; Musk floats adding customer-owned cars in December

Tesla launched unsupervised Robotaxi rides in San Antonio (9 Nov) — its second new city in two weeks after Fort Worth — and says the service now runs 24/7 in every available city. On 16 Nov Musk said Tesla could begin testing customer-owned vehicles on the platform as soon as December. Texas registry data shows Tesla and Waymo now neck-and-neck (~1,300 vehicles each). (expansion and registry counts from secondary coverage; “24/7” and the December timeline are Tesla/Musk claims)

Teslarati

Infineon and Qualcomm tie up on vehicle computing for centralised E/E architectures

Infineon and Qualcomm signed a technology agreement to expand the Snapdragon Ride ecosystem for centralised vehicle computers, jointly developing a microcontroller-based subsystem to run alongside Snapdragon Ride and Ride Flex. It’s a concrete signal of how safety MCUs and high-performance SoCs are being paired for the software-defined vehicle — squarely in the MBD/embedded lane. (announced ~3 Nov; agreement/LOI stage, not shipping silicon)

India · Cars & Bikes

India EV sales hit a record in October — electric cars up 76% YoY, two-wheelers lead

FADA data put total EV retail at a record ~3.55 lakh units in October (≈11.5% of all vehicle sales). Electric car retail jumped ~76% YoY to 37,049 units, with Tata and Mahindra together taking ~64% of the electric-car market; electric two-wheelers hit a record ~1.44 lakh units. (datasets differ slightly — FADA 37,049 vs Autocar India 36,911 electric cars; full November figures due early December)

Autocar India

GST cuts cool EV momentum as cheaper petrol/diesel cars surge in November

Autocar Pro reports that September’s GST cuts have narrowed the price gap between EVs and ICE vehicles, pulling early-November demand toward cheaper petrol and diesel models even as passenger and commercial EVs hold up — a reminder that EV adoption here is still highly price-elastic. (early-November trade read; firm November retail data not yet published)
