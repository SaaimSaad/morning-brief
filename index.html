Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; board rules out reinstating him and opens a formal CEO search

Huang resigned as president, CEO and director effective 12 November; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO (keeping the CFO role) and the board has retained Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent chief. On the 17 November earnings call the board confirmed it “does not intend to reinstate Jensen,” while Kress lifted the FY2028 revenue-growth outlook to ~75% year-on-year. (official via Nvidia’s newsroom and its SEC 8-K; the company has not given a reason — and aggregator claims circulating this week that Huang was being reinstated are false)

Automotive & MBD

Mercedes-Benz brings hands-free Level 2 highway driving to the US — and lays out its redundancy path to Level 3

Mercedes is rolling out hands-free highway driving with point-to-point navigation in the US, starting with a California pilot in December 2026, and classes the features as Level 2. Its MB.DRIVE ASSIST PRO platform — launching in Stuttgart and Munich by year-end — is the stated route to Level 3, which it says requires fully redundant steering, braking and power. (that redundancy is the functional-safety crux of eyes-off L3; Mercedes has also quietly dropped L3 from the latest S-Class)

WardsAuto

China pushes L3/L4 while Western OEMs retreat to “Level 2+”

Volkswagen has secured Level 3 test approval in China and Xpeng has begun Level 4 robotaxi demos, even as BMW and Mercedes pull eyes-off Level 3 from their flagship cars. Analysts tie the Western pivot to Level 2+ to liability transfer, high development cost and regulatory friction. (VW/Xpeng item dated late October; the L2+ vs L3 split is the strategic backdrop for any OEM ADAS programme)

India · Cars & Bikes

Toyota launches India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid — Innova Hycross facelift from ~₹19.99 lakh

The facelifted Innova Hycross pairs Toyota’s 2.0-litre petrol strong-hybrid powertrain with an engine certified to run on high ethanol blends (reported E20–E85), at an introductory ~₹19.99 lakh (ex-showroom) for the flex-fuel hybrid variant. It is a genuine powertrain-engineering first for India and a live test of ethanol strong-hybrids at scale. (now launched; outlets differ on the exact launch date and some earlier reports cited E100 — treat the final E-rating and variant pricing as still settling)

Tata Motors crosses 6 lakh passenger-vehicle sales in a calendar year for the first time

Tata recorded 6,49,477 PV wholesales in January–October 2026 — its first calendar year past 6 lakh — including a record 73,894 units in October. On the current run-rate it could close 2026 near 7.9 lakh, with EVs a major contributor. (wholesale/dispatch figures; November and December sales are still to report)
