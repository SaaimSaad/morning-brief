AI & LLMs

Anthropic files its IPO prospectus — $16.3bn revenue, $80.7bn loss

On 26 October Anthropic filed a public S-1 with the SEC. First-half 2026 revenue was $16.3bn (Q2 alone ~$11.6bn), with ~$5.6bn of Q2 spend on compute. The $80.7bn net loss is mostly a non-cash fair-value change on convertible notes, not operating cash burn. It is the first hard financial disclosure from a frontier lab and sets up a possible listing in the coming weeks.

OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra, a cheaper model for computer-use tasks

GPT-6.1 Terra is a faster, mid-tier model aimed at routine “computer use” and agentic workflows, replacing GPT-5.6 Terra in OpenAI’s line-up. Roundups put API pricing near $1 per million input tokens and $5 output — treat the exact figures as reported until OpenAI’s own pricing page confirms them. The trend worth noting: capable models keep getting cheaper for high-volume automation.

thenextweb.com

Sweden steers public-sector staff off ChatGPT and Claude toward Copilot (reported via AI-news roundups)

Industry digests report Sweden is directing government staff away from ChatGPT and Claude toward Microsoft Copilot, citing information-security concerns over where data is processed. A reminder that in regulated and safety-critical settings, tool choice is driven as much by data-governance and procurement as by raw model capability — relevant to anyone wiring AI into an engineering org.

Automotive & MBD

India moves to mandate automatic emergency braking in all cars

MoRTH’s draft notification (dated 19 October) would require Advanced Emergency Braking Systems on new car models (M1) from 1 October 2030 and on all models from 1 October 2032, conforming to standard AIS-185. A separate track mandates electronic stability control (AIS-162) and a revised braking standard for trucks from 1 October 2027. The draft is open for 30 days of public comment.

newsonair.gov.in

Mercedes-Benz and Nvidia expand their automated-driving partnership

Announced 16 October: the two will co-develop next-gen ADAS and automated driving, pairing Mercedes’ MB.OS with Nvidia’s stack — Hyperion (compute and sensors), Alpamayo (driving software) and Halos (safety) — with SAE Level 4 as the stated goal. Mercedes plans to offer an urban driver-assist system in select German cities from the end of 2026. No vehicle models or investment figures were disclosed.

Waymo crosses 20 million trips and lines up a Tokyo launch

Waymo said on 20 October it had passed 20 million cumulative paid trips and is running hundreds of thousands of rides a week. Its freeway service now operates in Austin — the fifth US city on highways, after Phoenix, San Francisco, LA and Miami — and it has finalised an agreement with Nihon Kotsu and GO to bring Level 4 robotaxis to Tokyo by 2027, subject to Japanese regulatory permits.

India · Cars & Bikes

Hyundai Bayon launches at ₹9.99 lakh

Hyundai confirmed pricing for the Bayon on 26 October, starting at ₹9.99 lakh ex-showroom — below pre-launch estimates. The 4.2m crossover uses the Creta’s 1.5-litre MPi petrol with manual and automatic options across trims. Hyundai framed it as the start of its next product cycle in India; it slots between the Venue and Creta, with no diesel or turbo at launch.
