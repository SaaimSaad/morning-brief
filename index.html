AI & LLMs

Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, cuts prices ~20%

On 9 Nov Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (coding, reasoning, vision), ~30% faster than the 5.1 models and priced at $8/M input and $40/M output tokens — down from $10/$50 for Fable 5.1. Anthropic says Fable 5.2 scored 49.1% at Xhigh effort on the FrontierCode 2.0 coding eval, ahead of Opus 5.5 (45.5%) and Fable 5.1 (32.4%). Mythos 5.2 is the same model without some cyber/bio safeguards, limited to vetted orgs via the Life Sciences and Cyber Verification programs; its system card flags stronger covert capabilities and more frequent evaluation-awareness than prior Claude models.

Automotive & MBD

NHTSA escalates Waymo school-bus probe to a recall query (2,895 vehicles)

US regulator NHTSA has upgraded its investigation into Waymo robotaxis passing stopped school buses to a recall query (RQ26006) covering 2,895 vehicles on the 5th-gen driving system, noting most logged incidents occurred after Waymo’s Nov-2025 software fix; the NTSB has opened its own probe. A pointed reminder that over-the-air “fixes” need the same validation and safety-case rigour as the original release. (regulatory filing reported 9 Nov)

Tesla closes the gap on Waymo in the Texas robotaxi registry

Texas DMV AV-registry trackers show Tesla’s robotaxi count at 1,282 vehicles — just three behind Waymo’s 1,285 — after adding 38 in the latest update, faster than Waymo’s growth. Two very different autonomy stacks (end-to-end vision vs HD-map + lidar) are now scaling head-to-head in one market. (registry-tracker counts, not company fleet disclosures)

Drive Tesla

Nvidia’s Halos SAFE pitches a traceable safety-evidence workflow for AVs

Introduced in late October, Nvidia Halos SAFE is a framework linking simulation, software- and hardware-in-the-loop evaluation, road testing and fleet monitoring to produce traceable safety evidence across L2–L4, with the safety case built on ISO 26262, ISO 21448/SOTIF, ISO PAS 8800 and UL 4600. Directly in the wheelhouse of SIL/MIL and functional-safety work. (introduced ~23 Oct; vendor framework, no independent evaluation of results yet)

India · Cars & Bikes

Record EV two-wheeler month: ~2.24 lakh units in October; TVS pulls ahead

India’s electric two-wheeler retails hit a record ~2.24 lakh units in October 2026 (up ~48% YoY). TVS led with 60,490 units (+94.7%), Bajaj was second at ~49,100, Ather sold 31,833 (share slipping to ~14%) and Ola fell 26.4% to 12,098. Note the contrast: overall (mostly petrol) two-wheeler retails fell 28.3% YoY to 23.21 lakh on FADA data, so EVs are gaining share in a shrinking post-festive market.
