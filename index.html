foxnews.com

Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a real-time “world model”

Announced 27 October, Genie 4 generates interactive environments from a prompt in real time, with simulated physics and human motion — a sandbox for training and testing AI agents rather than a chatbot. Reviewers note the physics still aren’t fully consistent. For anyone thinking about simulation-driven development, it is a notable step toward learned, generative test environments.

thenewstack.io

OpenAI keeps pushing AI-generated math into peer review

OpenAI’s drive to publish machine-generated mathematics continues: an earlier batch put out 722 manuscripts (372 result families) from an unreleased model touching 100+ open problems, many with Lean-formalized proofs, and roundups this week report further results being handed to mathematicians for review. Worth watching, with a caveat: “AI-generated,” “Lean-checked” and “community-accepted” are very different bars, and prompts/compute were not released.

Automotive & MBD

Elektrobit and Microchip pre-integrate Classic AUTOSAR base software

Directly in your lane: Elektrobit is packaging its EB tresos MCAL, AutoCore OS and AutoCore Generic modules pre-integrated onto Microchip’s SAMA5 automotive MPUs, with more platforms planned. The pitch is cutting repeatable Classic AUTOSAR integration effort via an “integration-as-code” approach the firms say could later support AI-assisted configuration — and initial market focus is Asia, including two-wheelers.

microchip.com

Waymo and Tesla both surge robotaxi coverage in one week

On 27 October Waymo began adding ~250 sq mi of driverless coverage across Los Angeles, Nashville, Orlando, San Diego and the Bay Area, citing 500,000+ paid rides a week and mapping Toronto for a 2028 launch. Tesla extended its Houston geofence to ~254 sq mi, added ~50 sq mi in Dallas, and listed San Antonio, Jacksonville, Las Vegas and Phoenix as next. Fleet and mileage comparisons remain contested between the two.

electrek.co

NHTSA opens an audit query into Tesla’s Cybercab safety compliance

The US regulator has opened an Audit Query into whether Tesla’s purpose-built Cybercab meets applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards, following a reported crash. A reminder that for driverless vehicles the homologation and functional-safety case — not just the autonomy stack — is where scrutiny lands as fleets scale.
