AI & LLMs

Meta ships frontier-class Muse Wonder, plus an open-weight Muse Spark

On 12 October Meta launched Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full — closed frontier models it claims lead on agentic, memory and coding tasks, pitched squarely against OpenAI and Anthropic. An open-weight Muse Spark followed on 15 October, keeping the top models closed.

Anthropic opens its models to independent evaluators

In a 15 October post, Anthropic said it is giving the nonprofit evaluator METR employee-like access to its frontier models, and is in early talks with others including Hugging Face — a step toward more external scrutiny of model capabilities and safety.

anthropic.com/news

Anthropic opens Singapore office, signs government MOU

On 14 October Anthropic opened a Singapore office and signed a memorandum of understanding with the Singapore government to advance safe, responsible AI adoption — continuing its Asia-Pacific build-out.

anthropic.com/news

Anthropic details election safeguards ahead of 2026 US midterms

A 14 October post lays out how Anthropic has tightened safeguards around election-related use of Claude before the US midterms — part of the wider governance push as frontier labs face scrutiny over political misuse.

Automotive & MBD

Subaru plans an in-house integrated ECU for software-defined vehicles

On 18 October Subaru detailed a move to software-defined vehicles later this decade, targeting roughly 40% shorter development time. It is building its own integrated ECU — handling ADAS, chassis, powertrain and infotainment on a custom AMD-co-developed chip — to enable OTA updates and AI in a future EyeSight generation, with production reported around 2029. Directly relevant to MBD/controls work at an OEM.

asia.nikkei.com

Toyota targets all ~50 models as software-defined by 2030

At an investor briefing, Toyota’s CTO said it aims to make its roughly 50 models software-defined by 2030 — about 10 million vehicles a year running its Arene software platform, with post-sale update revenue. Arene debuted in the 2025 RAV4.

timeslive.co.za

Mercedes-Benz and Nvidia expand their automated-driving partnership

The two expanded their tie-up, pairing Mercedes’ MB.OS and safety engineering with Nvidia’s Hyperion compute, the Alpamayo driving stack and the Halos safety layer — covering L2++ assistance now (the new CLA in the US) and aiming at future SAE Level 4. No L4 timeline or financial terms were disclosed.

group.mercedes-benz.com

Waymo drops waitlists in new US cities, heads to Canada

Waymo opened its robotaxi service to all riders (no waitlist) in markets including San Antonio, San Diego and Tampa, and announced its first Canadian deployment in Toronto, per TechCrunch’s 16–18 October coverage; it now operates in roughly 15 markets.

techcrunch.com

India · Cars & Bikes

Mahindra XUV 3XO CNG launched from ₹9.99 lakh

Mahindra’s factory-fitted CNG XUV 3XO opened for bookings and deliveries on 20 October, priced ₹9.99–12.27 lakh (ex-showroom) across four trims. It runs a 1.2L turbo-petrol on CNG with a claimed 24.7 km/kg, taking on the Tata Nexon and Maruti Brezza CNG.

drivespark.com

Locally built 2026 Audi Q3 launched at ₹51.99 lakh

Audi launched the all-new, India-assembled Q3 on 16 October from ₹51.99 lakh (ex-showroom) for the Nova trim — a 2.0 TFSI petrol (204 bhp) with quattro all-wheel drive, built at its Aurangabad plant.

newsbytesapp.com

Toyota Glanza facelift launched from ₹6.99 lakh, adds Level-2 ADAS

The 2026 Glanza facelift (the Maruti Baleno’s rebadged sibling) launched on 14 October from ₹6.99 lakh, now with a new 1.2L three-cylinder petrol, Level-2 ADAS, ventilated front seats and a tyre-pressure monitor.

timesdrive.in

Hero launches updated Xtreme 160R 4V and Xtreme 250R

Hero MotoCorp launched the 2026 Xtreme 160R 4V on 16 October at ₹1.27–1.41 lakh — adding ride-by-wire with three riding modes, cruise control and a TFT display on the top trim — alongside the Xtreme 250R at ₹1.98 lakh. Bookings are open; deliveries begin 1 November.
