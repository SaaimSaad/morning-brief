notateslaapp.com

Why ISO 26262 and ISO/SAE 21434 now have to be designed together

An Electronics Weekly piece (October) argues that cybersecurity (ISO/SAE 21434) and functional safety (ISO 26262) can no longer be separate workstreams: mechanisms like AUTOSAR’s SecOC add per-frame authentication and a freshness counter, and the article quantifies the real-time latency that crypto adds on a safety-critical braking ECU handling an ADAS camera command. Directly relevant for MBD teams budgeting timing on secured signal paths.

Electronics Weekly

Supplier compute consolidation: Magna widens NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion work for OEM deployments

Magna is expanding its collaboration with NVIDIA on the DRIVE Hyperion platform — contributing systems engineering, integration and validation while NVIDIA supplies the AI compute — to help automakers deploy software-defined driving stacks faster. It fits the broader shift (Mercedes’ CLA on DRIVE AGX with Level 2++ point-to-point assist) toward a few AI-compute platforms underpinning OEM autonomy roadmaps. (ongoing collaboration, not a new-this-week announcement)
