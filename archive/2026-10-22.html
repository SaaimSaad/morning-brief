AI & LLMs

OpenAI’s GPT-6 models arrive on Google Cloud

On 21 October OpenAI and Google said GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are entering Google Cloud’s Model Garden as a private preview, billed through the Cloud Marketplace. Google is now the only hyperscaler reselling all three leading closed families — OpenAI, its own Gemini, and Anthropic’s Claude — a notable truce between two direct rivals.

openai.com

Anthropic adds a physicist to its Long-Term Benefit Trust

In a 21 October post, Anthropic said observational cosmologist Christopher Stubbs — a former Harvard dean of science — has joined its Long-Term Benefit Trust, the body that holds a special class of stock and appoints board members to keep the mission ahead of commercial pressure.

anthropic.com/news

Anthropic commits shares to a new Public Benefit Fund

On 18 October Anthropic announced a Public Benefit Fund, pledging a portion of company equity to charitable purposes with a stake that grows as the company grows — a structural bet tied to its valuation rather than annual cash donations.

anthropic.com/news

Google ships Gemini 4 Argon for coding and agentic work

Released 13 October, Gemini 4 Argon is Google’s most capable model yet for complex coding, agentic, math and science tasks, per CNBC — rolled out cautiously, starting with cybersecurity partners. It is Google’s bid to close the frontier gap with OpenAI and Anthropic.

cnbc.com

Automotive & MBD

Elektrobit and Microchip pre-integrate Classic AUTOSAR software

Announced 15 October, the two are shipping pre-integrated AUTOSAR MCAL software on Elektrobit’s EB tresos for Microchip’s automotive microcontrollers, to cut OEM and Tier-1 integration effort. Elektrobit frames its “integration-as-code” approach to repeatable Classic AUTOSAR setup as the opening for AI-assisted configuration — squarely the MBD/controls workflow. Initial focus includes fast-growing Asian two-wheeler programs.

microchip.com

Nissan picks Valeo to lead next-gen ProPilot, with Wayve’s AI driver

Valeo has been named lead hardware and system supplier for Nissan’s next-generation ProPilot: its 360-degree sensor suite and central domain controller will run Wayve’s end-to-end “AI Driver” software as the decision layer. Valeo handles integration, validation and industrialisation; the first equipped model is due in Japan in FY2027.

just-auto.com

NHTSA opens a defect-petition review of Tesla robotaxis

US regulators opened file DP26010 on 9 October over a petition alleging Tesla robotaxis speed through school zones. It covers roughly 1,000 MY2026 Model Y and Cybercab units running the automated driving system; NHTSA has flagged four incidents (none with a crash or injury) and has until around 21 January 2027 to grant or deny a formal investigation.

autoguide.com

India · Cars & Bikes

Bajaj Pulsar N250SS launched at ₹1.59 lakh

Launched 21 October at ₹1,59,406 (ex-showroom Delhi), the N250SS moves the 249cc single to four valves (~25 PS/22 Nm) and adds a six-speed gearbox with a standard quickshifter (“Sports Shifter”), an electronic throttle, ride modes, a 5-inch TFT with Google Maps mirroring and a USB-C port — about ₹14,000 over the standard N250.

autox.com

Kawasaki Z300 launched at ₹3.24 lakh

Kawasaki launched the Z300 streetfighter in India on 21 October at ₹3.24 lakh (ex-showroom). It uses the Ninja 300’s 296cc liquid-cooled parallel-twin (~39 hp) with a six-speed box and adds a TFT console with smartphone connectivity, taking on the KTM 390 Duke.

autocarpro.in

Hyundai Bayon set for 26 October launch (upcoming — price not yet official)

Hyundai has confirmed a 26 October India launch for the Bayon crossover, with bookings open at ₹11,000. The India-spec car uses the Creta’s 1.5L naturally aspirated petrol with a 6-speed manual or IVT automatic. Price is unannounced; media estimates run roughly ₹9.5–15 lakh — treat as estimate until launch day.
