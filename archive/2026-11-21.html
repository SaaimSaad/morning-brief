AI & LLMs

Anthropic ends first week public near $1.3T — with a ~$70.7B compute bill coming due

After its record Nasdaq debut (closed +17% at $97.17 on 19 November), Anthropic enters its first week as a public company valued around $1.3 trillion. Filings show roughly $70.7 billion owed under compute commitments and leases for the 12 months to 30 Sept 2027; Q3 2026 compute and infrastructure spend was $9.9 billion, up 77% from $5.6 billion in Q2. (valuation varies by outlet: ~$1.3T on outstanding shares, up to ~$1.75T fully diluted)

GM leaves the NYSE after 115 years, starts trading on Nasdaq

General Motors moved its listing to the Nasdaq Global Select Market, with first trading as a Nasdaq company on 17 November under the unchanged ticker “GM.” GM framed the switch around its technology and software-defined-vehicle focus, and it opens eligibility for the Nasdaq-100. (ticker and shareholdings unchanged; a positioning move, not an operational one)

Aptiv introduces PRISM, an AI-enhanced ADAS platform for software-defined vehicles

Aptiv unveiled PRISM, a next-generation AI-enhanced ADAS platform built around an end-to-end AI stack for perception, fusion and behaviour planning on centralised compute — squarely the AI-in-embedded direction OEMs are pushing, where safety MCUs pair with high-performance AI SoCs. (platform announcement ~19 Nov, largely vendor claims; independent performance and ISO 26262 certification detail not yet public)

Festive hangover: India PV retail fell ~14% in October even as factory dispatches rose

FADA data show passenger-vehicle retail sales fell 14.23% YoY to 507,768 units in October 2026 (post-festive normalisation), while wholesale dispatches rose — Maruti alone shipped ~212,000 units, up ~20%. GST cuts have also tilted demand back toward ICE, cooling EV share. (October retail vs wholesale divergence; full November 2026 SIAM/FADA figures due mid-December)
