AI & LLMs

Anthropic files for a Nasdaq IPO, revealing $16.3bn first-half revenue

Anthropic’s S-1, filed 26 October, discloses revenue of $16.29bn for the six months to 30 June — up ~1,304% from $1.16bn a year earlier — and plans to list as “ANT.” It also booked large net losses (figures vary across reports) and left the IPO price range blank. Press reports put the targeted valuation at roughly $1.5–2tn, well above the ~$965bn post-money Series H in May; listing timing is reportedly late November but unconfirmed.

Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon takes #1 on Artificial Analysis’s Intelligence Index

Artificial Analysis says Gemini 4 Carbon is the new leader on its Intelligence Index v5 (28 October), overtaking the field. On the separate Coding Agent Index it still trails Claude Opus 5.5 — 59 (Carbon high, Antigravity CLI) vs 62 (Opus 5.5 max, Claude Code). Carbon was not yet generally available at announcement.

Major AI labs said to back a shared “opt-out” signal for training data (reported; not officially confirmed)

AI newsletters reported on 30 October that OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic will honour a single, standardised robots.txt-style token letting sites opt out of AI training — Anthropic said to have begun implementing it, OpenAI targeting “coming months.” I could not confirm this against the companies’ own pages, so treat it as reported rather than official.

Automotive & MBD

Waymo picks Toyota’s bZ4X as its next robotaxi, built at Takaoka

On 30 October Waymo and Toyota said Toyota will supply and help integrate a 2027 bZ4X carrying Waymo’s self-driving system, assembled at Toyota’s Takaoka plant in Japan, with testing in Japan next year and an eye on international markets including the UK. The pair’s earlier idea of putting autonomy into personally owned Toyotas remains exploratory, with no concrete plan yet — a notable OEM–AV supply tie-up rather than a consumer-car milestone.

TechCrunch

Mobileye trims full-year guidance as Q3 lands; shares slip

Mobileye’s Q3 2026 results (29 October) prompted a ~4.5% premarket drop even as it adjusted full-year guidance citing SuperVision strength. The EyeQ-based ADAS supplier remains a bellwether for how fast OEMs are committing to higher-level assisted and hands-off driving — worth watching for anyone tracking the ADAS production ramp.

Seeking Alpha

STMicroelectronics launches its first automotive safety combo sensors for mass-market use

On 26 October ST introduced combined accelerometer-plus-gyroscope (6-axis) inertial sensors in a single package, aimed at active and passive safety across price segments — the kind of ASIL-rated inertial sensing that feeds airbag deployment, stability control and ADAS functions in the safety-critical signal chain.

GlobeNewswire

Quiet on new Adaptive AUTOSAR / ISO 26262 announcements this weekend

No major new AUTOSAR platform release or OEM functional-safety milestone surfaced in the past day or two. The most MBD-relevant recent item remains Elektrobit and Microchip’s pre-integrated Classic AUTOSAR work (mid-October); the near-term watch items are the next AUTOSAR releases and OEM software reorganisations.

India · Cars & Bikes

Mercedes-Benz GLC Electric launches in India on 4 November

Bookings are open and a 4 November launch is locked for the locally assembled GLC Electric. India gets the dual-motor 400 4MATIC — around 360kW (~490hp) and 800Nm — off the Chakan (Pune) line. Price comes at launch; estimates run ~₹80–85 lakh (ex-showroom). It squares up against the BMW iX3 LWB due in 2027.

autocarindia.com

India’s electric two-wheeler registrations near 2.15 lakh in October

Vahan data shows 2,14,654 e-2W registrations in October, up ~2.9% month-on-month. TVS held the top spot and Hero posted the strongest growth among the leaders, while Ola Electric’s share slipped to 5.4% (from 6.4% in September). TVS, Bajaj, Ather and Hero together account for ~76% of the market this year.

Hero MotoCorp launched a Classique Edition of its Destini 110 petrol scooter on 30 October at ₹76,400 (ex-showroom, Delhi) — a trim/cosmetic special rather than a new platform or an EV.
