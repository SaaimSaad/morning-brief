AI & LLMs

Claude agents rewrote Anthropic’s production API in Rust — reported 40–70% less CPU

Anthropic migrated its Messages API backend — the path customers and its own apps (Claude Code, Cowork) use to reach Claude — from a Python monolith to Rust, with Claude generating most of the code while engineers owned design, verification and a shadow-traffic cutover. Coverage cites a rough 40–50% CPU reduction (some digests say ~70%) and 10–20% faster time-to-first-token in places. (rumour/unofficial: figures trace to a late-October Latent Space podcast and secondhand analysis, not a published Anthropic benchmark; one analysis argues Python’s inefficiency, not Rust alone, drove the gains)

OpenAI starts testing ‘Private Inference’ with enterprise customers

Sam Altman said on X that OpenAI has begun a limited test of Private Inference — a mode meant to keep enterprise prompts and data unreadable to OpenAI while they are processed — with a small group of business customers. (reported from newsletter digests dated 27 November; a limited pilot, not a general release)

Tesla passes 1,000 Cybercab registrations in Texas, leads Waymo on the state registry

Tesla added ~285 Cybercabs on Thursday to clear 1,000 registered in Texas; the state DMV now shows 1,841 vehicles registered for automated operation, with a tracker putting Waymo at ~1,409. Tesla also enlarged its Dallas robotaxi geofence to about 150 sq mi on 25 November. (registry counts, not active-fleet sizes; Tesla has not published a Dallas fleet number)

Waymo pauses driverless freeway rides nationwide as regulators probe edge cases

Waymo has stopped driverless freeway rides across the US after the California DMV barred it from all state freeways on 11 November; NHTSA is separately reported to be examining whether its vehicles reliably detect stopped school buses. These are exactly the ODD boundaries and perception edge-cases an L4 safety case has to close. (ongoing; freeway ODD and school-bus detection are the ASIL-weight scenarios a validation argument must cover)

Blockonomi

Tesla’s EU ‘Assisted Driving’ approval heads to a December vote

EU-wide type approval for Tesla Assisted Driving is expected to reach the TCMV committee in December; it needs at least 15 member states representing 65% of the EU population. So far 10 of 27 states have nationally recognised the Dutch RDW type approval (~14.3% of the population); Italy backs a swift approval while Sweden has pushed back. (homologation/type-approval mechanics; Musk has said he’d be surprised if EU-wide approval slipped past Q1 2027)

Royal Enfield Himalayan 750 launched at ₹4.89 lakh — its first twin-cylinder Himalayan

Unveiled at Motoverse in Goa, the 743cc parallel-twin adventure-tourer starts at ₹4.89 lakh (ex-showroom, cast wheels), rising to ₹5.14 lakh (spoked) and ₹6.29 lakh (touring flagship with GIVI luggage and heated grips). It makes 55 PS and 63 Nm, with cornering ABS, five ride modes and a 4-inch TFT. Bookings open for Motoverse attendees on 28–29 November and nationwide from 1 December. (introductory prices, ex-showroom; may change later)

Toyota Innova HyCross Flex Hybrid on sale — India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid

Toyota Kirloskar’s Innova HyCross Flex Hybrid is on sale from about ₹19.99 lakh (ex-showroom), pairing the 2.0-litre petrol strong-hybrid with an engine certified for high ethanol blends — a genuine powertrain-engineering first for India and a real-world test of ethanol strong-hybrids at scale. (a strong hybrid, not a BEV, despite the “EV” branding)
