AI & LLMs

Anthropic debuts on Nasdaq: opens +34%, closes +17% at $97.17, ~$1.27T valuation

Anthropic (ticker “ANT”) began trading Wednesday 19 November. Shares opened at $111.50, 34% above the $83 IPO price, then faded to close at $97.17 — up about 17% on the day — for a market value near $1.27 trillion. The IPO raised roughly $81.3 billion, the biggest on record. (valuation basis varies across outlets; an intraday market cap above $2T was quoted near the open before settling to the ~$1.27T close figure)

Forbes

Anthropic soars while the rest of Big AI slips — “circular deal” worries linger

On Anthropic’s debut day much of the AI-adjacent complex fell, with Alphabet, Meta, Amazon and SpaceX reportedly off between 2% and 4%. The split underlines ongoing unease about circular financing among the handful of firms that simultaneously fund, supply and buy from one another. (peer moves from debut-day coverage; a single day’s move, not a trend)

NPR

Nvidia posts a $115.8B quarter (+103%) — first report since Jensen Huang’s abrupt exit

For fiscal Q3 2027 (ended 25 Oct, reported 17 Nov) Nvidia booked $115.8 billion revenue, up 103% YoY, with data-center revenue of $107.6 billion (+110%) and Q4 guidance near $130 billion. It was the first set of results under interim CEO Colette Kress after co-founder Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November amid personal-conduct allegations. (earnings are official; Huang’s departure confirmed by multiple outlets, Nvidia gave no official reason; no permanent successor named)

Automotive & MBD

Waymo pulls driverless cars off freeways everywhere after California ban

Following a California DMV order barring its driverless vehicles from state freeways, Waymo suspended driverless freeway rides across all its markets. The episode — preceded this year by robotaxis entering active construction lanes and a near-3,900-vehicle software recall — is a pointed reminder that autonomy’s hard part is edge-case perception and regulatory validation, not the happy path. (details from secondary coverage; surface-street service continues)

Yahoo Finance

Tesla’s Cybercab fleet hits a record and retakes the Texas robotaxi lead from Waymo

Teslarati reports Tesla registered a record ~200 Cybercabs in Texas in a single day, pushing its purpose-built robotaxi fleet to ~773 vehicles and back ahead of Waymo. The two-seat Cybercab has no steering wheel or pedals; public rides remain centred on Austin, with wider Texas expansion underway. (registry counts and “lead” framing from secondary coverage; a reported Dallas Cybercab start is not independently confirmed)

Teslarati

Infineon unveils AURIX RC1, a RISC-V platform for software-defined vehicles and “physical AI”

Infineon’s new AURIX RC1 line (announced ~4 Nov) integrates up to 12 32-bit cores, a parallel processing unit for edge AI, and up to 40 MB of embedded memory — a safety-MCU building block for centralised E/E architectures, squarely in the MBD/embedded lane as OEMs pair safety MCUs with high-performance SoCs. (product announcement; a silicon roadmap, not yet in volume vehicles. Separately, chipmakers warn AI data-centre demand is squeezing mature automotive chip supply into 2028.)

India · Cars & Bikes

India EV retail up 45% YoY in October to ~3.55 lakh units; EV share near 9.7%

FADA data put total EV retail at 354,625 units in October 2026, up 45% YoY, with the EV share of all vehicle sales more than doubling to ~9.7% from 4.7% a year earlier. Electric two-wheelers rose ~48% to 224,064 units and electric passenger vehicles reached 37,049. (datasets differ slightly across outlets; full November figures due early December)

Autocar India

E-2W pecking order shifts: TVS and Bajaj surge, Ola slumps, Ather share slips

In October TVS led electric two-wheeler retail with 60,490 units and Bajaj took second with 49,126. Ola Electric was the only top-five brand to shrink — 12,098 units, down 26.4% YoY — while Ather’s share eased to ~14% from 20% a year earlier. Legacy ICE makers now clearly lead India’s electric two-wheeler race. (October retail data; monthly figures can swing with festive timing)

Toyota Innova Hycross facelift teased for a 25 November reveal — India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid?

Toyota has teased the Innova Hycross facelift ahead of a 25 November unveiling, expected at around ₹20.5 lakh. Reports flag it as potentially the first flex-fuel strong-hybrid car to launch in India — a notable powertrain milestone for the market. (RUMOUR on exact date/price — the teaser is official, but the launch date and flex-fuel claim come from outlet reporting, not a Toyota confirmation)
