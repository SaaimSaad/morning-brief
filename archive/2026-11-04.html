AI & LLMs

Anthropic nears its Nasdaq IPO as the S-1 reveals an $80.7bn half-year loss

On 3 November, CNN framed the imminent listing as the moment a company “founded to answer to humanity” starts answering to Wall Street. The S-1 filed 26 October (ticker “ANT”) discloses an $80.7bn loss for H1 2026 and more than 90 pages of risk factors, while separate reporting puts Q3 revenue near $17bn. A debut is expected later this month; no price or exact date is official yet.

CNN

Claude’s text watermarking is now live on older models — with a reported loophole

Anthropic’s SynthID-Text watermark is now applied across the older Claude line as of 3 November, about a month ahead of the EU AI Act deadline. It is applied globally (regional targeting isn’t yet reliable), and detection stays restricted to eligible organisations and regulators. MLQ reports the sampling-settings guidance isn’t attached to Mythos 5 or Opus 4.7 — a possible compliance gap on two models. A mark shows text came from Claude; its absence proves nothing.

MLQ

The compute bill behind the IPO: OpenAI trims a raise, Nvidia pitches container-sized data centres

A 3 November roundup notes OpenAI has reportedly cut its fundraising target by about $10bn amid market jitters over AI infrastructure spending, while Nvidia unveiled the “DSX POD,” a shipping-container-sized “AI factory” meant to stand up data-centre capacity in weeks rather than months. It is the capex backdrop to Anthropic’s ~$17bn-quarter revenue and nine-figure half-year losses.

Automotive & MBD

Tesla switches on unsupervised robotaxi rides in Fort Worth

Tesla launched Robotaxi in Fort Worth on 2 November with a 143-square-mile operating zone and, reportedly, unsupervised rides (no in-car safety monitor) — its first new city in over three months. Tesla also said Cybercab rides would reach Dallas “next month.” Consumer FSD remains supervised. Coverage flags that the exact start time and whether a monitor rode along were not fully clear.

Mobileye lands a high-volume “Surround ADAS” program with a major US OEM

Mobileye reported a high-volume Surround ADAS win with a major US automaker — a step up from front-facing assist to 360-degree perception using multiple cameras and radars around the vehicle. For an engineer eyeing an OEM role, it is a concrete read on where series ADAS content is heading: more sensors, more compute, tighter integration.

Industry backdrop: softening Tesla deliveries, Europe’s job squeeze

OICA’s 1 November digest flags Tesla EV deliveries set to fall on weak US demand, and warns that Europe’s automotive job losses are outpacing the new EV and software roles being created — a reminder that the software-defined-vehicle transition is also a workforce story.

MBD & functional safety: a quiet week, but Adaptive AUTOSAR keeps clearing ASIL bars

Nothing genuinely new surfaced this cycle on a fresh AUTOSAR release or an OEM functional-safety milestone. The standing storyline worth tracking: Adaptive AUTOSAR stacks (e.g. Qorix, ETAS RTA-VRTE) continuing to earn TÜV ISO 26262 certification up to ASIL-B/D, and suppliers such as Schaeffler and Sonatus pushing edge-AI into motion control — sharpening the open question of how to make non-deterministic AI safe inside ASIL-rated control paths. India · Cars & Bikes

October: India’s monthly PV wholesales cross 5 lakh for the first time

GST 2.0 cuts and Diwali demand drove a record festive month, with passenger-vehicle dispatches crossing 5 lakh units. Maruti Suzuki led with 2,12,384 (+20.5% YoY), Tata was second at 73,894 (+20.9%), and Mahindra posted a best-ever ~73,338 domestic SUVs. The industry read this as the real test after a frothy booking season — and it delivered.

Autocar India

But retail tells a cooler story — registrations down ~15% YoY

Vahan data counted ~4,67,494 registrations for October, down about 15% YoY even as it rose month-on-month. The gap between dispatches (to dealers) and registrations (to buyers) is the number to watch: wholesale records can mask inventory build-up if retail doesn’t follow. Mercedes-Benz GLC Electric launches in India today (4 November)

The Chakan (Pune)-assembled GLC 400 4MATIC — a 94kWh pack and a claimed 715km WLTP range — launches today as Mercedes-Benz India’s new electric SUV flagship. Price is revealed at launch; pre-launch estimates cluster around ₹84–85 lakh (ex-showroom). Its long-wheelbase BMW iX3 rival isn’t due until 2027.
