AI & LLMs

Claude Fable 5.2 tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

Anthropic’s Fable 5.2 reportedly leads the third-party Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 65, ahead of Google’s Gemini 4 (60) and Anthropic’s own Opus 5.5 (59) — putting Anthropic back at the frontier of the composite benchmark. The same coverage notes OpenAI has restricted its GPT-6 Cyber model to vetted security customers. (ranking is from one third-party index, not a universal measure of capability)

PYMNTS

Anthropic’s IPO roadshow targets a ~$1.4 trillion valuation

Anthropic has opened its IPO roadshow reportedly aiming for a valuation near $1.4 trillion and to raise as much as tens of billions, with a Nasdaq listing under ticker “ANT” expected the week of 16 Nov (ahead of Thanksgiving). Reported targets vary widely across outlets (roughly $1.02T–$1.4T); the last confirmed private mark was $965B (May 2026). (valuation and timing are reported pre-pricing; Anthropic hasn’t confirmed a final range)

Al Jazeera

Nonprofits sue OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta to halt frontier AI

A coalition of nonprofits filed suit on 12 Nov seeking to block further development of the most advanced (“frontier”) AI models by the major labs. Coverage reports OpenAI responded that it had paused training its most capable models pending additional safeguards. (newly filed complaint, no ruling; the OpenAI training-pause claim is reported and not independently confirmed — treat with caution)

Crypto Briefing

Automotive & MBD

Gemini Robotics 2.5 hits a claimed 96% on live Mercedes-Benz lines

Google DeepMind’s Gemini Robotics 2.5 vision-language-action model (introduced ~10 Nov), running on Apptronik’s Apollo 2 humanoids, sorted automotive parts on active Mercedes-Benz lines at Kecskemét (Hungary) and Sindelfingen (Germany) with a reported 96% success rate — picking metal brackets and placing them into per-vehicle sequencing containers. A concrete marker of learned, general-purpose control reaching real manufacturing. (proof-of-concept; 96% is a pilot figure, vendor-reported, with no detail on what the 4% miss means on the line)

Autocar

TIER IV open-sources end-to-end autonomous-driving AI models

TIER IV (11 Nov) released end-to-end autonomous-driving models designed to adapt to different map information and sensor configurations, published open-source via the Autoware platform. For an ISO 26262 / SOTIF engineer the open release is a useful reference for the hard part — building a safety argument around learned (vs rule-based) driving behaviour and its validation.

PR Newswire

ST and WITTENSTEIN demo mixed-criticality on Stellar G3 for SDVs

STMicroelectronics and WITTENSTEIN high integrity systems (10 Nov, at Electronica 2026) demonstrated mixed-criticality applications running on ST’s Stellar G3 automotive microcontrollers with SAFE RTOS, aimed at software-defined and zonal E/E architectures. Directly relevant to AUTOSAR partitioning and ASIL freedom-from-interference work as compute consolidates onto fewer, more powerful MCUs.

India · Cars & Bikes

BYD’s luxury brand Denza debuts in India today (13 Nov)

BYD brings its premium Denza marque to India today, led by the Z9 GT shooting-brake — a tri-motor EV with a claimed 1,156hp and a 270kph top speed — arriving as a fully-built import, with the D9 luxury MPV also expected to follow. Pricing is estimated at roughly ₹1.2–1.5 crore. (debut date official; price and full line-up reported/estimated, not yet confirmed by Denza)

Autocar India

TVS leads as India’s E2W sales nearly double year-on-year in October

TVS topped India’s electric two-wheeler sales in October 2026 as E2W volumes nearly doubled versus a year earlier. Even so, EVs’ share of the overall two-wheeler market slipped (to roughly 9.7% of retail) as GST cuts on petrol models revived ICE demand — a reminder that policy pricing, not just product, is steering the mix. (penetration figure is reported; final month data still firming up)

Autocar India

Toyota Innova Hycross facelift & Mini Cooper SE due later this month

The Innova Hycross facelift is expected around 25 Nov — reported as possibly India’s first flex-fuel strong hybrid, a direct nod to the ethanol-blend pathway — followed by the second-generation Mini Cooper SE on 27 Nov. A useful datapoint on how OEMs are hedging between full-electric and flex-fuel strategies under India’s fuel policy. (dates reported; not every launch timeline is officially confirmed)
